Scorekeeping

Teams record their own scores

Every net has a clipboard with that court’s printed schedule. After each game, write the final score on that sheet — it is the official record. The desk types the same scores into Scores here, and standings and the bracket fill themselves in.

Find your team

Pick your team to see your four pool games — round, time, court and opponent.

Full schedule

Courts 1–2 are Social, 3–4 Comp Pool A, 5–6 Comp Pool B — nobody changes courts during pool play. All six courts run Rounds 1–9; in Round 10 only Comp Pool A is still playing.

Court sheets print one court per page with blank score boxes — tape one to each net. The master is all 54 games on a single landscape page for the desk.

Teams

Live scoring

Enter each final as it comes off the sheet. Games are to 25, win by 2, hard cap 30. Scores save on this device and drive standings and the bracket.

Standings

Wins first, then point differential, then points scored. If two teams finish dead even, settle it head to head at the desk.

Bracket

Fills itself in the moment the last pool score lands. Tap a team to advance them.

How the day runs

Rounds are 25 minutes — a game to 25 plus changeover. Everything below is the plan, not a promise; the desk calls the real start of each round.
