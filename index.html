Scorekeeping

Teams record their own scores

Every net has a clipboard with that net’s printed schedule. After each game, write the final score on that sheet — it is the official record. An admin types the same scores in here, and standings and the bracket update live on everyone’s phone.

Find your team

Pick your team to see your four pool games — round, time, net and opponent.

Full schedule

Nets 1–2 are Men’s Pool A, 3–4 Men’s Pool B, 5 is Women’s Pool A and 6 is Women’s Pool B — nobody changes nets during pool play. All six nets run Rounds 1–8; the men wind down after that while the women play on to Round 10.

Net sheets print one net per page with blank score boxes — tape one to each net. The master is every game on a single landscape page for the desk.

Teams

Live scoring

Enter each final as it comes off the sheet. Games are to 21, win by 2, hard cap 25. Scores save on this device and drive standings and the bracket.

Standings

Wins first, then point differential, then points scored. If two teams finish dead even, settle it head to head at the desk.

Bracket

Fills itself in the moment the last pool score lands. Tap a team to advance them.

How the day runs

Rounds are 25 minutes — a game to 21 plus changeover. Everything below is the plan, not a promise; the desk calls the real start of each round.
